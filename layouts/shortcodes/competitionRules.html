Team Competition Rules

Team Composition
  • Each team must have a minimum of 3 runners.
  • Each team can have a maximum of 5 runners.
    • Team names should be family friendly
Categories
  • All Male
  • All Female
  • Mixed
Scoring System
  • Only 3 team members will score.
  • For mixed teams, at least 1 person from each sex will score.
  • Scoring will be based on total time. (lowest time wins)
Eligibility
  • All participants must be officially registered for the race.
  • Runners may only compete for one team.
Prizes
  • The team with the lowest cumulative time wins!
  • Best team uniform
  • Solidarity (first team where 3 members finish together)
  • All teams are eligible for random prizes
* Race organizers reserve the right to adjust rules if necessary.
