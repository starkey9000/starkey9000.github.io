## Team Relay Rules

1. Teams consist of 2 runners
2. There are 3 divisions: Female, Male, and Mixed
3. Division winners will receive a special award
4. First runner will cover 17 miles, and the 2nd runner will run 14
5. Exchange of the timing chip will take place in the aid station at mile 17
6. No batons necessary
7. Each team that finishes will receive a set of finishing awards
