1. Absolutely NO Transferring of bib numbers. Registered runners only! No pacers or bandits. This is critical to the safe tracking of all participants and the future of this event. If you are caught transferring or selling your bib, using a pacer or running as a bandit, you will be barred from future events.

2. Time Constraints will be STRICTLY enforced. A runner MUST LEAVE an aid station ahead of cut off times. If you do not make a cut off time, please hand bib number to course marshal, aid station captains, or race directors The aid station captain will see that you have a ride back to race HQ. Anyone who finishes the race after 9:00 hours or 5:30 PM will be listed on the official finish with No Time.

3. Race number must remain visible on the front torso at all times.

4. You must stay on an established trail and follow all course markings

5. Listen and comply with all marshals, event staff and park officials.

6. No littering. This should go without saying, but this is not a road marathon, do not throw your trash on the ground. The Maryland State Parks is kind enough to give us a permit to hold this event each year. Littering of any kind will make the permit process more difficult than it already is. In addition, you will be disqualified for littering on State Park property.

7. No race day signup.

8. No deferrals.

9. Hiking poles are allowed, however, interfering with another runner, intentional or not, will result in a disqualification. Please consider not using them until after the first 5k to allow runners a chance to spread out.

10. No Dogs for runners. Remind your guests, dogs must be leashed and not allowed in aid stations.

11. No Early starters. Race starts promptly at 0830, leaving before the official start is grounds for disqualification.

12. We strongly discourage the use of personal listening devices. If you do use them, then headphones only. No audible speakers. Also, we ask that you remain alert to other runners and race officials.
